offhrs — Tax & money flow (as built)

Ontario platform · Canada-only Marketplace goods · Stripe Connect · Product configuration as of Nov 2026

1 SaaS

Lite / Pro subscription

Monthly software fee for Partners (workshops tooling).

  1. Partner pays monthly fee Lite or Pro billed on Stripe.
  2. offhrs charges HST on SaaS Where applicable on the subscription.
  3. offhrs remits SaaS HST Platform obligation on the software fee.

Shopify Sync is $29 CAD/month after trial, billed via Shopify Billing (not Stripe). 0% commission on Shopify workshop checkouts.

2 Workshops

Workshop ticket sales

0% platform commission. Stripe card fees borne by the vendor.

  1. Buyer pays workshop ticket Checkout on offhrs via Stripe Connect.
  2. Tax depends on vendor CRA status Controlled in Partners → Settings → GST/HST registration.
If GST/HST-registered: Stripe Tax adds GST/HST → collected into the vendor Connect account → vendor remits.
If not registered: checkout does not add GST/HST.
3 Marketplace

Artist Marketplace goods

Ontario buyers only at launch. CPA Nov 2026: tax on all Marketplace sales.

  1. Buyer pays item + shipping + GST/HST Tax collected on every Marketplace sale (registered and unregistered sellers).
  2. Money split Item toward vendor Connect; 3% of item subtotal (ex-tax, ex-shipping) to offhrs — no separate HST on the 3% per CPA; shipping held by offhrs for Shippo / Canada Post labels.
  3. Who remits Marketplace tax Depends on seller CRA registration (see below). Tax code txcd_99999999 OK for now.
Registered seller: GST/HST into Connect → seller remits.
Unregistered seller: still collect tax at checkout → offhrs remits (CPA). Engineering gap vs older registered-only build.
CPA Nov 2026: Treat as closer to a distribution platform operator than a marketplace facilitator. Workshops stay registered-only (vendor remits). Marketplace = tax on all sales; unregistered → platform remits. PST: review by province / item type. Ensure GST for buyers outside Ontario.
Not tax advice. Reflects CPA direction Nov 2026 + product gaps called out above. Live terms: https://offhrs.app/terms/
