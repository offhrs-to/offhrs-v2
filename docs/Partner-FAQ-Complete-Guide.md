## 1. Getting started & onboarding

### How do I become an offhrs partner?

Sign up at **partners.offhrs.app** through the partner signup wizard. You will provide:

- **Business name** (2–100 characters)
- **Workshop categories** — choose 1 to 4 categories; your first selection is your primary category. Available categories: Beauty & Fragrance, Culinary, Coffee, Floral, Pottery, and Other. If you choose Other, you must add a short description (up to 200 characters).
- **Business location** — your primary in-person address, selected from the address search (this is used for your public profile and as the default location when you create workshops).
- **Account details** — email, password (minimum 8 characters), and optional phone number.
- **Optional:** website URL, workshop logo (JPEG, PNG, or WebP, up to 2 MB).

After you create your account, you must **verify your email**, then choose a plan and complete billing setup to access the full dashboard.

---

### Do I have to verify my email?

**Yes.** You cannot start a paid plan or complete billing until you confirm your email using the link we send you. Once verified, you are taken to the billing step to choose Lite or Pro and start your free trial.

If you already use the offhrs mobile app with the same email and your email was previously confirmed, verification may already be complete and you can proceed to billing sooner.

---

### What are the steps after I sign up?

Your dashboard includes a **Getting Started** checklist:

1. Verify your email  
2. Start your free trial (complete Stripe subscription checkout)  
3. Set up your Stripe payout account (Stripe Connect)  
4. Confirm your GST/HST status in Settings (registered with CRA, or small supplier)  
5. Review Settings and add your bio  
6. Connect a calendar (Google or Microsoft Outlook)  
7. Create your first workshop  

You can work through these in any order, but you need billing completed before the dashboard is fully unlocked, and you need Stripe Connect set up before payouts can be released to your bank account.

---

### I already have an offhrs consumer account. Can I use the same email?

**Yes.** The same email can be used for both the mobile app (as a customer) and the partner portal (as a vendor).

- If you sign up as a partner with an email that already has a consumer account **and you use the same password**, a vendor profile is added to your existing account.
- If the email exists but the password does not match, you will be asked to sign in with your existing password first.
- If you originally signed up with Google or Apple on mobile, you may need to set a password during partner signup; you will still need to verify your email before billing.

Deleting your **partner** account does **not** delete your consumer mobile account if you use the same email.

---

### Is there a partner mobile app?

**No.** The partner portal is **web-only** at partners.offhrs.app. It works on phones and tablets through your browser, but all partner management (workshops, bookings, payouts, settings) is done on the web.

Your **customers** book through the **offhrs mobile app** (iOS and Android).

---

### What information should I have ready before signing up?

- Your legal or public business name  
- A Canadian business address (for your profile and tax setup)  
- A credit or debit card for your subscription (required to start the 30-day free trial)  
- Government ID and banking details for **Stripe Connect** (you can complete this after signup; takes about 2 minutes)  
- Your GST/HST registration number, if you are registered with the CRA

## 2. Plans, billing & subscription

### What does it cost to list workshops on offhrs?

offhrs charges a **monthly subscription** — there is **no commission** on individual bookings. Two plans are available (all prices in **CAD**):

| Plan | Monthly price | Active workshops |
|------|---------------|------------------|
| **Lite** | $29 / month | Up to **4** active workshops at a time |
| **Pro** | $49 / month | **Unlimited** active workshops |

“Active” means published, draft, or fully booked workshops that are **not archived**. Archiving a workshop frees a slot on the Lite plan immediately.

---

### Is there a free trial?

**Yes — 30 days free.** You enter your card when you start, but you are not charged until the trial ends. You can cancel anytime during the trial through the billing portal and you will not be charged for that billing period.

When the trial ends, your subscription automatically converts to a paid plan (Lite or Pro — whichever you chose at signup) unless you cancel first.

You will receive an email reminder when your trial is about to end (approximately 3 days before).

---

### How do I choose between Lite and Pro?

- **Lite** is a good fit if you run a small number of workshops at once (up to 4 non-archived listings).  
- **Pro** is better if you run many concurrent workshops, recurring series, or seasonal offerings and do not want to archive old listings to add new ones.

You can upgrade from Lite to Pro at any time through **Settings → Manage billing** in the Stripe customer portal.

---

### How is my subscription billed?

- Billing is handled by **Stripe** (secure card processing).  
- Subscription checkout includes **automatic tax** and **tax ID collection** where applicable on your platform fee.  
- Your account status syncs automatically: trialing, active, past due, suspended, or canceled.

---

### How do I update my card or manage my subscription?

Go to **Settings** in your partner dashboard and click **Manage billing**. This opens the **Stripe Customer Portal**, where you can:

- Update your payment method  
- Switch between Lite and Pro (where available)  
- View invoices  
- Cancel your subscription  

---

### What happens if my payment fails?

If a subscription payment fails:

1. Your account may show as **past due** — you can still access the dashboard, but you will see a warning to update your card.  
2. You will receive an email asking you to update your payment method within **3 days**.  
3. If payment is not resolved, your account may be **suspended** and you will be redirected to a suspension page until billing is fixed.

Update your card in **Settings → Manage billing** to restore access.

---

### How do I cancel my partner subscription?

Cancel anytime through **Settings → Manage billing** (Stripe Customer Portal).

- If you cancel, you typically retain access until the **end of your current billing period** (or until the end of your trial if you cancel during the trial).  
- After cancellation, your account status becomes **canceled** and dashboard access is restricted.  
- Your data is retained for **30 days** after cancellation. Contact support if you need help exporting information before that period ends.

---

### Does offhrs take a commission on each booking?

**No.** offhrs does not charge a percentage fee on ticket sales. Our revenue comes from your monthly subscription only.

However, every paid booking is subject to the standard **Stripe payment processing fee** (approximately **2.9% + $0.30 CAD** per transaction in Canada). This fee is paid by you as the vendor. International or cross-border cards may incur higher processing fees — your dashboard shows the actual fee per booking when available.

## 3. Getting paid — Stripe Connect, fees & payouts

### How do I receive money from workshop bookings?

You are paid through **Stripe Connect (Express)** — a Canadian payout account linked to your offhrs partner profile.

**Setup steps:**

1. Log in to your partner dashboard.  
2. Go to **Payouts** (or follow the Getting Started checklist).  
3. Click to connect with Stripe and complete Stripe’s short onboarding (~2 minutes).  
4. Provide the identity and banking information Stripe requires for Canadian businesses.

Once connected, when a customer pays in the mobile app, the charge is processed on your behalf and funds are routed to your Stripe Express balance (after the Stripe processing fee).

---

### Do I need Stripe Connect before customers can book?

Customers **can book** even if you have not finished Stripe Connect setup. However, **payouts cannot be released** to your bank until Connect onboarding is complete. You will see a dashboard reminder until setup is finished.

Complete Connect as soon as possible so funds can flow to your account on Stripe’s normal payout schedule.

---

### When do payouts arrive in my bank account?

Stripe issues payouts on **Stripe’s standard payout schedule** for your Express account (typically a few business days after charges settle, depending on your account and bank). This is controlled by Stripe, not offhrs.

In your dashboard under **Payouts**, you can see:

- Payout amount (CAD)  
- Expected arrival date  
- Status (pending, paid, failed, or canceled)  

For full balance and payout details, use **Open Stripe Express** from the Payouts page.

---

### What fees are deducted from each booking?

For each paid booking, the customer pays:

- Your workshop ticket price  
- Plus applicable **GST/HST** (if you are registered and tax is enabled — see Section 4)  

From that payment:

- The **Stripe processing fee** is deducted (shown in your Bookings view as “Stripe fee”).  
- The remainder is your **net payout** (shown as “Net to you” or similar in the dashboard).

offhrs does not take an additional booking commission.

---

### What happens to the processing fee when I refund a booking?

**You absorb the original Stripe processing fee.** In Canada, Stripe does not return the processing fee when a charge is refunded. When a full refund is issued, the ticket amount is returned to the customer, but the processing fee you paid on the original transaction is not recovered. This is standard for Stripe in Canada and is disclosed in our terms and refund emails.

---

### Can I see a history of all payouts?

Yes. The **Payouts** section of your dashboard lists recent payouts (up to 50 records) with amount, date, and status. For complete history and tax documents, use the **Stripe Express Dashboard**.

## 4. GST/HST & sales tax

### Am I responsible for sales tax on my workshops?

**Yes.** As a partner, you are the **seller of record** for your workshop tickets. Sales tax (GST/HST) on ticket sales is your tax liability, not offhrs’s.

offhrs uses **Stripe Tax** on your connected Stripe account to calculate and collect tax at checkout when you are registered for GST/HST.

---

### How do I set up GST/HST in my dashboard?

Go to **Settings → Workshop sales tax (GST/HST)**. You have two paths:

**Option A — Registered with the CRA**

- Turn on “I am registered for GST/HST.”  
- Enter your registration number in the format: `123456789 RT 0001` (9 digits, RT, 4 digits).  
- Save. We attempt to configure Stripe Tax on your connected account using your business address.

**Option B — Small supplier (not registered)**

- Turn off GST/HST registration and save.  
- Use this if you are below the CRA small-supplier threshold (generally under **$30,000** in taxable sales over four consecutive calendar quarters).  
- No GST/HST will be charged on your workshop tickets until you register and update Settings.

Confirming your tax status also completes a Getting Started checklist item.

---

### When is tax shown to customers?

- On workshop listings in the app, customers see your **base ticket price** plus a note such as **“Tax calculated at checkout.”**  
- The full breakdown (subtotal, tax, total) appears in the payment screen immediately before they confirm payment.  
- Tax is calculated from the customer’s Canadian province/postal code at checkout.  

**Canada only** — all provinces and territories are supported.

---

### Do I charge tax on free workshops?

No sales tax applies when the ticket price is **$0** (free workshops).

---

### Is tax charged on my offhrs subscription (Lite/Pro)?

Your **monthly offhrs subscription** is billed separately through Stripe Checkout with automatic tax and tax ID collection where applicable. This is separate from GST/HST on workshop tickets you sell to customers.

## 5. Creating & managing workshops

### How do I create a workshop?

Go to **Workshops** in your dashboard and click to add a new workshop. You can save as **Draft** (not visible to customers) or **Published** (live and bookable).

---

### What fields can I set on a workshop?

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Title | 2–120 characters |
| Description | Up to 2,000 characters |
| Category | One of: Beauty & Fragrance, Culinary, Coffee, Floral, Pottery, Other |
| Price | $0–$10,000 CAD ($0 = free) |
| Capacity | 1–500 attendees |
| Duration | 15–480 minutes |
| Date & time | Single session or multi-week series |
| Location | **In-person** (address) or **Virtual** (meeting link URL) |
| Cover image | Optional; uses your default workshop image if omitted |
| External bookings | Optional count of spots already booked outside the app |

Your default in-person address from signup pre-fills the location field; you can change it per workshop.

---

### What is the difference between in-person and virtual workshops?

- **In-person:** Enter a physical address. Customers see the location in the app. Use a full street address for best results.  
- **Virtual:** Enter a valid meeting URL (Zoom, Google Meet, etc.). Customers receive the link after booking (per your workshop setup).

---

### Can I run multi-session or recurring workshops?

**Yes.** Workshop scheduling supports:

**One-day workshop**  
A single date and time.

**Multi-week series (2–12 sessions)**  

- **Cohort (same group every week):** One booking reserves a seat across **all** sessions in the series. Available as “weekly same time” or “weekly custom times.”  
- **Drop-in / per-occurrence:** Repeating on selected weekdays over 2–12 weeks; availability is managed per occurrence.

Choose the pattern that matches how you run your class (fixed group vs. open drop-ins).

---

### What do workshop statuses mean?

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Draft** | Not visible to customers; useful while you are still editing. |
| **Published** | Live and bookable in the mobile app. |
| **Fully booked** | Set automatically when all spots are taken. |
| **Archived** | Soft-deleted; hidden from customers; active bookings are refunded (see Section 6). |

---

### What happens when my workshop sells out?

When available spots reach zero, the status changes to **Fully booked** automatically. You receive an email notification. The workshop remains visible but cannot accept new bookings until you increase capacity or reduce external booking counts.

---

### Can I track bookings made outside the app?

**Yes.** Use the **“Already booked elsewhere”** (external booked count) field when creating or editing a workshop. This reduces in-app availability so you do not overbook. You can set 0–500 external spots.

---

### I am on the Lite plan and cannot add another workshop. What do I do?

Lite allows **4 active (non-archived) workshops** at a time. To add another:

- **Archive** a workshop you no longer need listed, or  
- **Upgrade to Pro** through Settings → Manage billing.

---

### Can I edit a workshop after it is published?

Yes. You can update most fields from the Workshops section. Be careful changing date, time, or capacity when bookings already exist — communicate with attendees if schedules change significantly.

---

### How do I remove a workshop from the app?

**Archive** the workshop. This hides it from customers, removes it from your connected calendar, and automatically refunds active bookings (see refunds section). Archiving cannot be undone from the dashboard — contact support if you archived by mistake.

## 6. Bookings, clients & refunds

### Where do I see my bookings?

Go to **Bookings** in your dashboard. You will see attendee name, email, workshop, amount paid, Stripe fee, net payout to you, and booking status.

Filter by: confirmed, cancelled, refunded, or pending.

---

### Can I export booking data?

**Yes.** Use **Export CSV** on the Bookings page. The export includes subtotal, tax, total, Stripe fee, and net vendor amounts suitable for your records or accountant.

---

### What is the Clients page?

**Clients** aggregates everyone who has booked your workshops:

- Name, email, phone (when the customer has a linked mobile profile)  
- Workshops enrolled, booking count, first enrollment date  
- Customer reviews (rating and comment), when left  

You can search, sort, and filter (e.g., by workshop, has review, has phone). Results are paginated at 25 per page.

---

### What is the refund / cancellation window?

Each vendor sets a **refund window** — how many hours before a session starts a customer can cancel for a full refund.

- **Default:** 48 hours before the session  
- **Minimum (platform policy):** 24 hours  
- **Maximum:** 8,760 hours (1 year)  

Change this in **Settings → Refund window**.

Customers who cancel **before** the cutoff receive an automatic full refund. After the cutoff, the app tells them refunds are no longer available and to contact you directly.

---

### Can I issue a refund manually?

**Yes**, from the **Bookings** page. Select a booking and issue a full refund. Manual refunds follow the same refund window rules as customer cancellations (you cannot refund inside the window unless you contact support for exceptional cases).

You will be asked to confirm before the refund is processed.

---

### What happens to bookings if I archive a workshop?

When you archive a workshop, **all active bookings are automatically refunded**, even if the session is inside the normal refund window. If any refund fails, the archive may be blocked until the issue is resolved.

---

### Who gets notified when someone books or cancels?

You receive email notifications for:

- New bookings (attendee, workshop, amount)  
- Fully booked workshops  
- Refunds on your workshops  

The dashboard **notifications bell** also shows recent activity (new bookings, refunds, workshop published, reminders, tax settings prompts). It refreshes periodically while you are on the dashboard.

---

### Can customers leave reviews?

**Yes.** Signed-in customers can leave **one review per vendor** (rating and optional comment). Reviews appear on your public vendor page in the mobile app and in your Clients list.

## 7. Calendar sync

### Can I sync workshops to my personal calendar?

**Yes.** Connect **Google Calendar** or **Microsoft Outlook** from the **Calendar** section of your dashboard.

**How it works:**

- **Published** and **fully booked** workshops with a date/time create events on your connected calendar.  
- **Draft** and **archived** workshops are removed from your calendar.  
- Multi-week series create **one calendar event per session date**.  
- Default timezone is **America/Toronto**.

Sync is **from offhrs to your calendar** (outbound). Workshops you create in offhrs appear on your calendar; importing events from an external calendar into offhrs is not supported.

---

### Do customers get calendar invites?

Customer calendar behavior is handled through the mobile app booking flow. Your connected calendar is for **your** scheduling visibility.

## 8. Your customers & the mobile app

### Where do customers book my workshops?

In the **offhrs mobile app** (iOS and Android). All in-app payment (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay), booking history, and cancellations for partner-listed workshops happen in the app.

---

### Why do workshop links on the website open an app download page?

Public workshop URLs on offhrs.app/workshops are **landing pages** that encourage app download. If someone shares a direct workshop link, they will see the workshop name and a prompt to get the app. **Booking and payment always happen in the app** for partner workshops.

---

### Do I have a public vendor page?

**Yes.** Customers can view your **vendor page** in the app with your business name, bio, average rating, reviews, and upcoming workshops.

Keep your **Settings** profile (bio, logo, website, phone) up to date — this is what customers see.

---

### What is the difference between my workshops and other listings on offhrs?

Workshops you create as a partner are **vendor-listed**: bookable and paid in the app through your Stripe account.

Some older or manually entered listings on the platform only link out to an external website for booking — those do not process payment through offhrs. As a partner, your listings are the in-app, paid kind.

## 10. Account settings, security & deletion

### What can I update in Settings?

- Business name, bio, website, phone, location address  
- Refund window (hours before session)  
- GST/HST registration status and number  
- Password  
- Subscription and billing (via Stripe portal)  

---

### How do I reset my password?

Use **Forgot password** on the partner login page, or change your password in **Settings** when logged in.

---

### How do I delete my partner account?

In **Settings**, use the account deletion option. This will:

- Cancel your partner subscription immediately  
- Delete your vendor profile, workshops, bookings, payout records, and calendar connections  

If you use the same email for the mobile consumer app, **your consumer account is preserved** and you can continue using the app as a customer.

## 11. Troubleshooting & edge cases

### I verified my email but I am stuck on the billing page.

Make sure you completed **Stripe Checkout** for Lite or Pro. Until checkout succeeds, your account stays in **pending** status and full dashboard access is limited. If checkout failed or was canceled, return to the billing step and try again.

---

### My dashboard says “past due.”

Your last subscription payment failed. Go to **Settings → Manage billing** and update your payment method. Once payment succeeds, your status returns to **active**.

---

### My account is suspended.

Subscription payment failed repeatedly or your subscription was canceled. Update billing in Settings or contact **support@offhrs.app**. Suspended accounts cannot use the dashboard until billing is resolved. Data is retained for 30 days.

---

### Stripe Connect says incomplete.

Finish onboarding from **Payouts**. You need valid identity and bank details for a Canadian Express account. If Stripe requests additional verification, complete it in the Stripe Express dashboard.

---

### Tax is not being charged on my workshops.

Check **Settings → GST/HST**:

- You must mark yourself as **registered** and enter a valid registration number, **and**  
- Save successfully (Stripe Tax must sync to your connected account).

If you are a small supplier and turned registration off, tax will not be charged — which is correct for unregistered vendors under the small-supplier rules.

---

### A customer says they cannot cancel for a refund.

They are likely **inside your refund window** (too close to the session start). The app shows how many hours before the session refunds are allowed. You can:

- Handle it case-by-case outside the platform, or  
- Adjust your refund window in Settings for **future** bookings (existing bookings follow the policy in effect at booking time).

---

### I need to change the date of a workshop with existing bookings.

Edit the workshop carefully and notify attendees directly (email or message). Large schedule changes may warrant issuing refunds and asking customers to rebook — contact support if you need guidance.

---

### Can I use offhrs if I am outside Canada?

Partner signup and Stripe Connect are configured for **Canadian** vendors and CAD pricing. Consumer booking and tax support are **Canada only** at this time.

---

### Who do I contact for help?

**Email:** support@offhrs.app  

Include your business name, workshop title (if relevant), and screenshots when possible. For payout or tax document questions, you may also use the **Stripe Express Dashboard** linked from Payouts.
